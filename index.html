Our Story

A modern ode to old Singapore

Housed within a restored 1920s shophouse row on the edge of the historic Straits waterfront, Straits Muse Hotel is a love letter to the traders, artisans and dreamers who shaped this island.

Every suite is a gallery in its own right — hand-glazed Peranakan tiles, bespoke rattan and teak, and original works by Southeast Asian artists — softened by the hush of a truly personal service.

Eleanor Tan
General Manager
