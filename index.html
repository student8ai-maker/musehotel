Selamat Datang

An island within an island

Step through our carved teak gates and the city fades behind you. Water gardens murmur, frangipani scents the air, and thatched pavilions open wide to the breeze.

Every detail is handmade: volcanic stone from Java, alang-alang thatch, batik woven by local artisans. The service is warm and unhurried, in the Balinese spirit of tri hita karana, harmony between people, nature and spirit.

Terima kasih, and welcome.
